# AI LAB HUB Marketplace Rules

Version of [MP_RULES_VERSION]

## 1. General provisions

1.1. These Rules set out the terms for posting and viewing listings in the Marketplace section of the website ailabhub-directory.com (the “Platform”).

1.2. The Platform is owned by Serena Milova, a private individual (the “Administration”). Contact details are given in section 13.

1.3. The Marketplace is a classifieds board. The Administration only provides space for information about goods, services, courses and jobs. The Administration is not a seller, buyer, employer, contractor or agent of any party and does not take part in deals between users.

1.4. Users do not pay each other through the Platform. They contact each other using the details the listing author chose to provide.

1.5. By posting or viewing listings, a user confirms that they have read and accept these Rules, the Platform’s [Terms of Use](terms.php) and [Privacy Policy](privacy.php). Submitting a listing requires a separate consent tick in the form.

1.6. If the language versions of these Rules differ, the Ukrainian version prevails.

## 3. Who may post listings

3.1. Anyone may view listings. The Author’s contact details are shown only to registered users, to protect them from automated harvesting and spam.

3.2. Listings may be posted by registered users who are at least 18 years old or act with the permission of their parents (guardians), and by organisations through their authorised representatives.

3.3. The Author must give true information about themselves. Impersonating another person, company or brand is prohibited.

3.4. The Author is responsible for the security of their account and for everything published from it.

3.5. The Administration may limit the number of listings per Author and require confirmation of an email address or phone number.

## 4. Permitted listing topics

4.1. The Marketplace accepts only listings related to artificial intelligence and digital technologies:

- **Ready-made AI solutions** — paid or free;
- **Courses and training** — online and offline;
- **Jobs and job search** in AI and related fields;
- **Services** — AI implementation, setup, consulting, content creation with AI.

4.2. A listing is posted in the matching category. A wrong category is a reason to move or reject the listing.

4.3. One listing — one offer. Duplicate listings are prohibited.

4.4. A listing must describe the offer honestly: what exactly a person gets, at what price, when, and with what limitations. If a price is given, it must be the full price, without hidden extra charges.

4.5. If images, video or text in a listing are AI-generated and may be taken as real (photos of results, reviews, people), the Author must say so.

## 5. Prohibited content

5.1. The following may not be posted in the Marketplace:

1. listings unrelated to the topics in section 4;
2. goods and services whose circulation is banned or restricted by law: weapons, narcotic and psychotropic substances, prescription drugs, unlicensed gambling;
3. fraudulent schemes, financial pyramids, promises of guaranteed income;
4. hacking tools, malware, databases of other people’s personal data, stolen accounts or access keys;
5. services creating deepfakes of real people without their consent, and intimate images of anyone;
6. pornography and any sexual content involving minors;
7. services writing theses, taking exams or tests for another person, forging documents;
8. fake reviews, likes, followers, fake accounts;
9. selling other people’s products, courses, characters or images without the right to do so (piracy, reselling accounts and subscriptions against a service’s rules);
10. calls for violence, discrimination or hatred on any grounds;
11. content calling for the elimination of Ukraine’s independence or a change of its borders, or justifying armed aggression against Ukraine;
12. hidden links, redirects to phishing sites, demands to prepay for “registration” or “training materials” for a job;
13. other people’s personal data and photos of people without their consent.

5.2. This list is not exhaustive. The Administration may reject any listing that breaks the laws of Ukraine, infringes third-party rights or threatens users’ safety.

## 6. Special requirements for jobs and courses

**Jobs**

6.1. A job may be posted by the employer or its representative. A job listing states: the employer’s name, position, duties, qualification requirements, format (office/remote) and, for on-site jobs, the place of work.

6.2. Job listings may not set requirements or preferences based on age, sex, appearance, race, ethnic origin, religion, marital status, having children, disability or other characteristics unrelated to the work, except where the law expressly provides otherwise.

6.3. Candidates may not be asked to pay for an interview, training, a test task or access to work.

6.4. A training course, multi-level marketing or the sale of services may not be presented as a job.

**Courses**

6.5. A course listing states: who the teacher or organiser is, the programme, duration, format (online/offline, city), full price and refund terms.

6.6. A course may not promise guaranteed employment, income or a state-recognised education document unless the organiser is entitled to issue one. A non-formal education certificate must be called exactly that.

## 7. Moderation

7.1. Every new listing and every significant change is moderated before publication. The usual time is up to [MP_MODERATION_DAYS] business days.

7.2. Moderation checks that a listing complies with these Rules. It does not confirm the quality of the offer, the truth of all of the Author’s statements or the Author’s good faith.

7.3. The Administration may:

- reject a listing or return it for changes, stating the reason;
- move a listing to another category;
- hide or remove a published listing if a breach is found or a well-founded complaint is received;
- temporarily or permanently restrict an Author from posting, or block the account, for repeated or serious breaches.

7.4. For serious breaches (fraud, items 5.1.4–5.1.6 and 5.1.11) the account is blocked without warning. In other cases the Author first receives a warning.

7.5. The Author may appeal a moderation decision within [MP_APPEAL_WINDOW_DAYS] days in their account or by email to [MP_ABUSE_EMAIL] with a link to the listing. We answer within [MP_APPEAL_ANSWER_DAYS] business days.

7.6. A listing is automatically unpublished after [MP_LISTING_LIFETIME_DAYS] days and can be extended by the Author.

8.1. All arrangements and deals (purchases, services, training, employment) are made directly between users, without the Administration.

8.2. The Author alone is responsible for:

- the content of the listing and its compliance with the law;
- the quality, safety and legality of their goods, services, courses and jobs;
- holding the rights to the materials posted;
- paying taxes on their income and registering their business where the law requires it.

8.3. The Administration does not check or guarantee the reliability of Authors, the fulfilment of their promises or the result of a deal. The Administration is not liable for losses from deals between users, except where such liability is expressly provided by law and cannot be limited by contract.

8.4. The Administration does not guarantee that the Marketplace will work without interruption and may temporarily restrict access for maintenance.

8.5. The Author compensates the Administration for losses and costs arising from third-party claims about the content of their listing.

## 9. Intellectual property and protection of authors

9.1. By posting a listing, the Author confirms that they hold the rights to all texts, images, video, characters and trademarks in it.

9.2. The Author keeps their rights. The Administration receives a free non-exclusive licence only to show the listing on the Platform, translate it, create thumbnails and add watermarks. The licence lasts while the listing is published.

9.3. To protect authors, the Platform may show images at reduced quality, with watermarks, and limit saving them. These measures do not guarantee full protection against copying.

9.4. Users may not copy, distribute or sell materials from other people’s listings without the Author’s permission. This includes automated collection of Marketplace data (scraping, collection for AI training).

9.5. If the Author sells or gives away a digital product (templates, characters, images), they set the terms of use in the listing (for example, “personal use only”, “commercial use allowed”, “no modifications”).

9.6. The AI LAB HUB brand, logo, the assistant character Eli and the Platform’s design belong to the Administration. Using them in listings without permission is prohibited.

## 10. Rights holder complaints

10.1. If a listing infringes your copyright or other rights, send a notice to [MP_ABUSE_EMAIL] or use the “Report” button. In the notice, state:

1. your name or the name of your organisation and contact details;
2. a link to the infringing listing;
3. which work or item is infringed;
4. proof of your rights (a dated original, publication, certificate, contract);
5. a statement that the information is true and you are acting in good faith.

10.2. The Administration reviews a properly completed notice within [MP_IP_REVIEW_DAYS] business days. If the infringement is obvious, the listing is hidden during the review.

10.3. The Author is notified of the complaint and may submit a counter-notice with proof of their rights within [MP_COUNTER_NOTICE_DAYS] business days. If no counter-notice is received, the listing is removed.

10.4. If the parties provide conflicting evidence, the Administration does not decide the dispute on the merits. The listing may stay hidden until a court decision or an agreement between the parties.

10.5. For repeated confirmed infringements the Author’s account is blocked. Knowingly false complaints are not considered, and their senders may be blocked.

## 11. Complaints about listings and safety

11.1. You can report a listing (fraud, prohibited content, misleading description) with the “Report” button on the listing page or by email to [MP_ABUSE_EMAIL].

11.2. Tips for safe deals:

- do not pay in full in advance to strangers, especially for “employment” or “access”;
- never share CVV codes, SMS codes, passwords or API keys;
- do not follow links to “pay via AI LAB HUB” — the Platform does not accept payments for users’ listings;
- for services and courses, sign a written agreement or keep the correspondence with what was agreed.

11.3. In case of fraud, contact the police and your bank. The Administration keeps data about the listing and the Author’s account and provides it to law enforcement upon a lawful request.

## 12. Personal data and showing contact details

12.1. By posting a listing, the Author agrees that the contact details they entered in the listing form may be shown to registered users. The consent lasts while the listing is published. By deleting the listing or a contact detail, the Author withdraws the consent.

12.2. A user who receives an Author’s contact details may use them only to get in touch about that listing. Collecting contact details for mailings, resale or spam is prohibited.

12.3. Views of contact details are logged (who viewed them and when) to prevent abuse. The Administration may limit the number of contact views per day.

12.4. In all other respects, personal data is processed under the [Privacy Policy](privacy.php) and the Law of Ukraine “On Personal Data Protection”. Do not publish unnecessary personal data in a listing (home address, ID document details, card number).

13.3. The Administration may change these Rules. A new version is published on this page with its date. Authors are notified of significant changes [MP_RULES_CHANGE_NOTICE_DAYS] days before they take effect.
