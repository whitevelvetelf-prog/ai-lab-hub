# AI for Psychology and Mental Health: The Complete Comparison

AI is increasingly used in mental health — both as a tool for everyday self-support and as a working assistant for psychologists and therapists, handling the routine documentation that comes with every session. In this article, we compare 13 such services, grouped into two categories: self-support apps and tools for professionals.

## Step 1: Self-Support Apps

These services are built for everyday users — helping track mood, keep a journal, meditate, or simply talk to an AI companion.

### [Wysa](https://ailabhub-directory.com/product.php?id=1142)

An anonymous AI assistant built on evidence-based approaches: unlimited conversations, guided exercises, and support between sessions with a human therapist. Separate programs are available for employers and healthcare organizations.

**Pricing:** for organizations — price on request.

**Best for:** people who want support for their emotional wellbeing, as well as employers and healthcare organizations.

### [Replika](https://ailabhub-directory.com/product.php?id=1174)

An AI companion for everyday conversation and emotional support: it remembers details about your life, offers a customizable appearance and personality, and supports voice calls. The service is intended for adult users (18+).

**Pricing:** pricing is not published on the product's site.

**Best for:** adults looking for a companion for everyday conversation.

### [Calm](https://ailabhub-directory.com/product.php?id=71)

One of the most popular meditation and sleep apps — over 150 million downloads, with guided meditations and Sleep Stories narrated by well-known voices.

**Pricing:** Free — limited content; Annual — $69.99/yr; Lifetime — $499.99 one-time.

**Best for:** anyone who wants to reduce everyday stress or improve sleep quality.

### [Headspace](https://ailabhub-directory.com/product.php?id=1143)

A meditation app featuring Ebb, an AI companion that listens via voice or text and personally recommends meditations and exercises for you.

**Pricing:** 14-day free trial, then a monthly or annual subscription.

**Best for:** people who want less stress, better sleep, and a regular meditation practice.

### [Earkick](https://ailabhub-directory.com/product.php?id=1238)

An AI mental-health tracker with a panda companion: mood tracking plus a free AI support chat with no sign-up required.

**Pricing:** free.

**Best for:** people who want to keep track of their mood and have support on hand.

### [Mindsera](https://ailabhub-directory.com/product.php?id=1239)

An AI journal for mental clarity: analyzes your emotions, spots recurring themes in your entries, offers more than 50 mental models, and provides a Big Five personality assessment.

**Pricing:** Curious — free, basic journal; Genius — $14.99/mo (or $129/yr), full emotion and voice analysis.

**Best for:** people who keep a journal and are working on self-awareness.

### [Rosebud](https://ailabhub-directory.com/product.php?id=1144)

An AI journal for personal growth: helps you process emotions through guided follow-up questions, spots patterns in your entries, and supports habit-building.

**Pricing:** Free — basic use at no cost; Bloom (monthly) — $12.99/mo; Bloom (annual) — $107.99/yr (≈$8.99/mo).

**Best for:** people who keep a journal and work on personal growth and emotional wellbeing.

### [Ash (Slingshot AI)](https://ailabhub-directory.com/product.php?id=1237)

An AI conversational companion built specifically for mental health: 24/7 support via text or voice, memory of your history, and weekly personal insights.

**Pricing:** pricing is not published on the product's site.

**Best for:** adults looking for support with stress, anxiety, or relationship topics.

## Step 2: Tools for Psychologists and Therapists

These services aren't built for the end client — they're built for professionals, automating session documentation, treatment plans, and practice administration.

### [Upheal](https://ailabhub-directory.com/product.php?id=1240)

AI notes and an electronic health record for therapists: automatic session notes, treatment plans, teletherapy, and practice management, built to HIPAA standards.

**Pricing:** Free — basic AI notes for individual therapy; AI Notes — $39/mo, unlimited notes; EHR Pro — $99/mo, full record with an AI assistant.

**Best for:** therapists, psychologists, and counselors in private practice.

### [Mentalyc](https://ailabhub-directory.com/product.php?id=1241)

AI documentation for therapists: records sessions and produces notes in SOAP, DAP, and BIRP formats, and helps with treatment plans. Meets HIPAA and SOC 2 standards.

**Pricing:** Mini — $19.99/mo, 40 notes; Basic — $39.99/mo, 100 notes; Pro — $69.99/mo, 160 notes.

**Best for:** therapists, clinical psychologists, and social workers.

### [Blueprint](https://ailabhub-directory.com/product.php?id=1242)

An AI assistant for therapists: notes and treatment plans, client letters, session prep, and billing and insurance — used standalone or alongside your existing health record system.

**Pricing:** free to start, further pricing on the product's site.

**Best for:** therapists and mental health practices.

### [Lyssn](https://ailabhub-directory.com/product.php?id=1243)

AI for quality and training in mental healthcare: analyzes session recordings for adherence to evidence-based methods and gives clinicians feedback to develop their skills.

**Pricing:** price on request, for organizations.

**Best for:** mental healthcare organizations, supervisors, and training programs.

### [Limbic](https://ailabhub-directory.com/product.php?id=1244)

A clinical AI for mental healthcare services: agents for intake, triage, and ongoing therapeutic support. Registered as a Class IIa medical device in the UK and used within NHS Talking Therapies.

**Pricing:** price on request, after a demo.

**Best for:** mental healthcare services, clinics, and insurance plans.
