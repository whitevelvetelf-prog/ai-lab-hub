# AI for Language Learning: The Complete Comparison

Learning a new language is no longer limited to textbooks and audio courses. AI technology now lets you practice daily through gamified exercises, work on pronunciation with a smart tutor, or find a live tutor online — all in one app. In this article, we compare six popular language-learning services, grouped by learning format: from basic gamified apps to conversational practice with AI and real people.

## Step 1: Basic and Gamified Language Learning

These services are a great fit for beginners, or for anyone who wants to keep their existing skills sharp with short daily exercises.

### [Duolingo](https://ailabhub-directory.com/product.php?id=29)

Duolingo teaches languages through short, gamified exercises: sentence translation, listening drills, and AI-powered conversation practice. The app tracks your progress and nudges you to come back daily through its "streak" system. It works equally well for absolute beginners and for practicing skills you already have.

**Best for:** anyone who wants to start learning a language from scratch or maintain a daily practice habit without any financial commitment.

### [Busuu](https://ailabhub-directory.com/product.php?id=326)

Busuu offers structured courses in more than 14 languages, from beginner to advanced level, built by professional methodologists. Its standout feature is feedback on writing and pronunciation exercises from real native speakers in the community, complemented by AI-powered conversations. It also includes offline mode and level certificates.

**Best for:** learners who value an expert-designed course structure along with genuine feedback from native speakers, not just an algorithm.

### [Babbel](https://ailabhub-directory.com/product.php?id=77)

One of the most popular paid language-learning services — structured short lessons with speech recognition, used by more than 10 million subscribers.

**Pricing:** 1 month — $17.95/mo; 12 months — $7.99/mo (best value, access to one language); Lifetime — $299.99 one-time, lifetime access to all languages.

**Best for:** learners ready to invest right away in a quality structured course, who value speech recognition for pronunciation control.

## Step 2: Conversational Practice with AI and Live Tutors

Once the basic grammar and vocabulary are in place, the next step is speaking. These services focus specifically on conversational practice.

### [Speak](https://ailabhub-directory.com/product.php?id=317)

AI-powered conversational language practice built on a "Learn → Practice → Apply" method, with an AI tutor that responds in real time and gives instant feedback.

**Pricing:** 7-day free trial with full access; Premium — $17.99/mo; Premium annual — $83.99/yr (significantly cheaper when paid annually).

**Best for:** learners who want to build speaking confidence with an AI conversation partner available anytime.

### [TalkPal AI](https://ailabhub-directory.com/product.php?id=320)

A cloud-based, GPT-powered AI tutor for learning more than 57 languages, built around affordable and flexible practice across different conversation modes.

**Pricing:** Free — 10 minutes of practice per day at no cost; Premium (annual plan) — $9.99/mo with a 50% discount; Premium (monthly plan) — $19.99/mo, unlimited practice across all modes.

**Best for:** learners looking for the most budget-friendly entry point into AI conversational practice, across multiple languages at once.

### [Learna](https://ailabhub-directory.com/product.php?id=339)

An AI tutor for conversational practice in English and Spanish: a virtual chat character gives instant feedback on grammar and pronunciation, with lessons that adapt to the learner's level and goals from the very first session.

**Pricing:** 7-day free trial, followed by a paid subscription (exact pricing varies by region — see the product page for current rates).

**Best for:** learners who want to start speaking English or Spanish at their own pace, without the fear of making mistakes.

### [Preply](https://ailabhub-directory.com/product.php?id=335)

An online platform for finding tutors in more than 90 languages for one-on-one private lessons. You pay per lesson, with pricing set by each individual tutor.

**Pricing:** tutoring sessions from $15.00, with exact pricing depending on the tutor (typically $3–50+/hour).

**Best for:** learners who value real human connection and a personalized lesson plan, rather than AI-only practice.

## Which Tool Should You Choose?

- **Just starting out and want to try it for free** — start with [Duolingo](https://ailabhub-directory.com/product.php?id=29).
- **Want course structure and native-speaker feedback** — go with [Busuu](https://ailabhub-directory.com/product.php?id=326).
- **Ready to invest in a full course with pronunciation control** — [Babbel](https://ailabhub-directory.com/product.php?id=77).
- **Main goal is speaking with confidence** — [Speak](https://ailabhub-directory.com/product.php?id=317), [TalkPal AI](https://ailabhub-directory.com/product.php?id=320), or [Learna](https://ailabhub-directory.com/product.php?id=339), depending on your budget and target language.
- **Want a live tutor and a personalized plan** — [Preply](https://ailabhub-directory.com/product.php?id=335).
