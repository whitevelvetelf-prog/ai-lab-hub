## The AI you are already using without noticing

The most widespread AI in games is upscaling and frame generation. According to NVIDIA at CES 2026, [DLSS 4 with Multi Frame Generation](https://www.nvidia.com/en-us/geforce/news/dlss-4-rtx-path-tracing-game-announcements-ces-2026/) is supported in more than 250 games and apps. Among the most-played games of 2025 on that list are ARC Raiders, Battlefield 6, Clair Obscur: Expedition 33 and Where Winds Meet. The technology also works in Cyberpunk 2077, Alan Wake 2 and Hogwarts Legacy.

The idea is simple: the graphics card renders some of the frames and a neural network fills in the rest, so the game runs smoother even with ray tracing. At CES 2026 NVIDIA showed DLSS 4.5 with a new Super Resolution model and a 6X Dynamic Multi Frame Generation mode.

AMD has its own answer, FSR 4. At Computex 2026 the company confirmed that it is [supported in more than 300 games](https://www.tweaktown.com/news/111868/amds-fsr-4-passes-a-new-milestone-ai-powered-super-resolution-in-300-plus-games/index.html). At first it was only for Radeon RX 9000 cards (RDNA 4), but AMD promises FSR 4.1 for older generations too. Intel also has XeSS 2.

Three things worth knowing:

- Multi Frame Generation only works on RTX 50 graphics cards. RTX 40 cards get regular Frame Generation (up to 2x).
- Smooth picture and fast response are not the same thing: generated frames do not take your input.
- Not every game supports every technology, so check the settings of the specific game.

## A teammate who never leaves voice chat: PUBG Ally

This one already looks like the future. Krafton and NVIDIA built [PUBG Ally](https://developer.nvidia.com/blog/how-krafton-built-pubg-ally-a-co-playable-character-powered-by-nvidia-ace/) for PUBG: Battlegrounds. It is a "Co-Playable Character": not an ordinary bot, but a character that understands your voice, reads the situation in the game and replies with words and actions. It can suggest strategy, share loot, drive vehicles and fight other players.

At CES 2026 Krafton showed Ally with long-term memory, and from June 17 to July 1, 2026 it ran a beta of the Duo mode on the Sanhok map. Whether Ally becomes a permanent feature is not known yet, so follow PUBG updates.

Other games are heading the same way. NARAKA: Bladepoint got an [AI teammate that runs locally on your PC](https://www.nvidia.com/en-us/geforce/news/nvidia-ace-autonomous-ai-companions-pubg-naraka-bladepoint/), and MIR5 plans bosses that adapt to your tactics.

## inZOI: a city where residents have goals of their own

inZOI is a life sim in the spirit of The Sims from Krafton, which launched on Steam on March 28, 2025. It has an experimental feature called [Smart Zoi](https://www.nvidia.com/en-gb/geforce/news/nvidia-ace-naraka-bladepoint-inzoi-launch-this-month): city residents get life goals, and their thoughts and actions are driven by a small NVIDIA ACE language model that runs right on your PC. To enable it you need a GeForce RTX graphics card. According to NVIDIA, this makes the everyday life of your characters more engaging and closer to real life.

## Games where you can just talk to NPCs

Forget the menu with three answer options. [In these games, characters respond to free text or voice](https://arcanumrpgs.com/blog/games-with-ai-npcs/):

- **Where Winds Meet** is the biggest game on this list, where some characters talk to you in free language.
- **Suck Up!** is a comedy where you play a vampire and use your voice to talk AI residents into letting you into their homes.
- **Vaudeville** is a whodunit where every townsperson's dialogue is generated live. You can interrogate them by typing or speaking.
- **Verbal Verdict** is a 1950s detective game (in early access) built around interrogating suspects controlled by AI.
- **Whispers from the Star** and **AI Roguelite** are two more released games where NPCs answer free text or speech.

## The Darth Vader who was tricked into swearing: a Fortnite lesson

In 2025 Epic Games added a Darth Vader you can talk to in Fortnite. The lines were generated by the Gemini 2.0 Flash model, and [James Earl Jones's voice was recreated by ElevenLabs with permission from his family](https://www.techradar.com/computing/artificial-intelligence/forget-the-force-ai-brings-the-late-james-earl-jones-iconic-darth-vader-voice-to-fortnite). To talk to him, you need to turn on the Conversational AI feature in the settings and recruit Vader. Players under 13 cannot talk to him without parental permission.

The outcome was predictable: almost immediately, [players tricked AI Vader into swearing](https://www.pcgamer.com/games/battle-royale/fortnite-added-an-ai-powered-darth-vader-and-surprise-players-immediately-tricked-him-into-saying-slurs/), and Epic pushed a hotfix. The actors' union SAG-AFTRA criticized the practice itself, because it takes work away from actors who could have voiced the character.

## Skyrim that finally answers back: Mantella

If you still play Skyrim or Fallout 4, here is your main AI mod: Mantella. You speak into a microphone, the mod recognizes your speech, asks a language model and voices the reply in the NPC's voice. According to the project page, you can talk to more than 3,000 NPCs, each with their own backstory. They remember previous conversations, know about events in the game and can even see what you see.

The mod is free and open source. You can run everything locally on your own PC or connect OpenAI, OpenRouter or any compatible API. The project page reports more than 350 thousand downloads. For Minecraft there are similar community mods, such as Player2 AI NPC.

To be honest: the quality of conversations depends on the model you pick and on your hardware, and one reviewer noted that the idea is still better than the execution. But it is worth trying, especially if you love role-playing games.

## Worlds on demand: Project Genie

On January 29, 2026, Google opened [Project Genie](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Project_Genie_%28website%29), an experimental prototype built on DeepMind's Genie 3 world model (introduced in August 2025). You describe a world in text or upload a photo, and you can simply walk around in it. The world is generated on the fly as you move, at about 24 frames per second. It also stays consistent: if you go back, a new area will not appear on top of the old one.

It sounds like the future of games, but that future is still far away. Access is limited to Google AI Ultra subscribers (in the US at launch). Early testers wrote about a limit of roughly a minute per world. There is no API and no export, and you cannot make a full game this way. According to its developers, Genie's main focus is robotics and simulation, not games. Even so, media reports say that after the launch the shares of several game publishers fell: investors seem to be watching this technology closely.

## A coach in your pocket: Mobalytics, Aimlabs and more

For competitive players, AI works as a coach:

- **Mobalytics** analyzes your matches and shows your weak spots. It covers League of Legends, Valorant, TFT, Marvel Rivals and other games from one account. There is a free tier and a paid subscription.
- **Aimlabs** is an AI aim coach. According to its website, more than 40 million players use it, it has more than 50 thousand tasks and profiles for 500 FPS games, and Valorant and Rainbow Six Siege are official partners. The AI finds your weak spots and builds personal training routines.
- **[Blitz.gg](https://ailabhub-directory.com/product.php?id=1627)** shows hints right during a match, and **[iTero.gg](https://ailabhub-directory.com/product.php?id=1654)** is built for League of Legends players.

## Chat without toxicity: AI guarding the voice

Not all AI in games is entertainment. Activision, together with the company Modulate, added [a system called ToxMod](https://gamesbeat.com/how-toxmods-ai-impacted-toxicity-in-call-of-duty-voice-chat-case-study/) to Call of Duty. It listens to voice chat, picks out the most likely rule violations and passes them to human moderators for review. According to the companies, exposure to toxicity in voice chat dropped by 50% in Modern Warfare II and Warzone in North America. And in Modern Warfare III (worldwide except Asia), Activision took action against two million accounts that broke the rules in voice chat.

You can also protect your own voice from noise yourself: [Krisp](https://ailabhub-directory.com/product.php?id=165) removes background noise during calls in real time, and [Voicemod](https://ailabhub-directory.com/product.php?id=183) changes your voice on the fly for streams and games.

## Hints without alt-tab: Gaming Copilot and G-Assist

Stuck on a boss or not sure where to go next? Gaming Copilot from Microsoft is a voice assistant that gives tips and recommends games based on your history. It works in the Xbox Game Bar on Windows 11, in the Xbox mobile app and on the ROG Xbox Ally handheld, and [Microsoft promises it on Xbox Series X|S consoles later in 2026](https://winbuzzer.com/2026/03/16/microsoft-gaming-copilot-ai-xbox-consoles-2026-xcxwbn/).

[NVIDIA G-Assist](https://ailabhub-directory.com/product.php?id=2919) is a local AI assistant that runs right on GeForce RTX graphics cards, helps optimize settings and gives tips.

## The honest downsides

- Not everything shown at trade shows has reached mainstream games yet.
- Privacy. According to media reports, Gaming Copilot was enabled by default on its first platforms and sent text from screenshots to Microsoft for model training. Check the settings and turn training off if you do not want that.
- Ethics and labor. The story of AI Vader's voice showed that voice actors are wary of such technology.
- Moderation. Conversational AI characters can say things their developers did not expect, as happened with Vader in Fortnite.
- Conversations with AI characters depend on the chosen model and your hardware.
- Prices and hardware requirements change, so check current terms on each website.
- Many games on Steam have an AI usage disclosure on their store page. Read it before you buy.
