# AI for Drinks: Cocktails, Coffee, Wine, Beer and Running a Bar

A few years ago a "smart" drink meant little more than a coffee maker with a timer. Today an app suggests a cocktail from what is on your shelf, tells you how to change the grind for your next espresso and reads a restaurant wine list from a photo. In bars AI tracks every pour, and in some coffee shops robots already make the drinks. Here is what actually works and how to choose a tool for your task: at home, as a barista or as a bartender.

All the tools in this article are collected in the "Drinks" subcategory of the "My Home" section of our catalog.

## In short: where AI already helps with drinks

- **Cocktails at home:** recipe generators based on the ingredients you have.
- **Non-alcoholic drinks:** mocktails, lemonades, smoothies and sparkling water.
- **Coffee:** AI guidance for espresso and roasting.
- **Wine:** AI sommeliers, label scanners and cellar tracking.
- **Beer:** brewing apps and fermentation sensors.
- **Spirits:** whisky bottle scanners and collection tracking.
- **Running a bar:** inventory, pour control and sales forecasting.
- **Automation:** robot bartenders, robot baristas and self-serve kiosks.

## Cocktails at home: what to make from what you have

The most popular scenario is simple: you tell the app what you have, and it shows what you can make. [Cocktail Builder](https://ailabhub-directory.com/product.php?id=3254) shows every cocktail you can make from your bar for free and remembers what is in it. [Mixel](https://ailabhub-directory.com/product.php?id=3242) and [Cocktail Flow](https://ailabhub-directory.com/product.php?id=3241) sort recipes by what you have at hand, and [Mixology](https://ailabhub-directory.com/product.php?id=3237) also suggests ingredient substitutions and a shopping list.

If you want something new, AI generators can help. [Mixologia](https://ailabhub-directory.com/product.php?id=3239) invents a cocktail for your ingredients and mood and keeps a "My Bar" section. [dr.inks](https://ailabhub-directory.com/product.php?id=3240) has about a thousand recipes and creates your own to match your taste, and the Spanish-language [GuIA del Cóctel](https://ailabhub-directory.com/product.php?id=3238) offers an AI cocktail for your party theme.

For inspiration and technique there are guides and media: [Difford's Guide](https://ailabhub-directory.com/product.php?id=3243) with thousands of recipes, [Liquor.com](https://ailabhub-directory.com/product.php?id=3251), [PUNCH](https://ailabhub-directory.com/product.php?id=3250), [Imbibe Magazine](https://ailabhub-directory.com/product.php?id=3256), [VinePair](https://ailabhub-directory.com/product.php?id=3258), [Kindred Cocktails](https://ailabhub-directory.com/product.php?id=3257) and [Cocktails Distilled](https://ailabhub-directory.com/product.php?id=3260). Developers will find [TheCocktailDB](https://ailabhub-directory.com/product.php?id=3252) useful, with its free API.

You can go further and automate the process itself. [Barsys](https://ailabhub-directory.com/product.php?id=3245) offers a smart cocktail maker, a shaker and a scale coaster, and the free Barsys AI app works even without a device. [Bartesian](https://ailabhub-directory.com/product.php?id=3246) makes cocktails from capsules, and [MyBar](https://ailabhub-directory.com/product.php?id=3247) from your own bottles; its case can be 3D printed. If you do not want to hunt for ingredients, there are delivered kits: [Shaker & Spoon](https://ailabhub-directory.com/product.php?id=3248) and [Cocktail Courier](https://ailabhub-directory.com/product.php?id=3249).

## Non-alcoholic drinks

A non-alcoholic drink can be interesting too. [Sharbat](https://ailabhub-directory.com/product.php?id=3253) has more than 500 recipes for mocktails, juices, tea, coffee and smoothies, and [dr.inks](https://ailabhub-directory.com/product.php?id=3240) has a separate section for mocktails and lemonades. [Shaker & Spoon](https://ailabhub-directory.com/product.php?id=3248) offers a zero-proof version of its kits. [SodaStream](https://ailabhub-directory.com/product.php?id=3327) carbonates water at home and shares mocktail recipes, [Cirkul](https://ailabhub-directory.com/product.php?id=3328) adds flavor to water, and [Vitamix](https://ailabhub-directory.com/product.php?id=3329) is for smoothies and juices. For fermented drinks there is [Kombucha Kamp](https://ailabhub-directory.com/product.php?id=3324) with kombucha and kefir recipes, and [Teafinity](https://ailabhub-directory.com/product.php?id=3322) identifies tea from a photo and times your steeping.

## Coffee: an AI assistant for baristas

Dialing in espresso always takes a few tries. Now AI helps with that. [Dial In](https://ailabhub-directory.com/product.php?id=3274) logs dose, yield, time and taste and tells you which way to move the grind for the next shot. [Pucky](https://ailabhub-directory.com/product.php?id=3277) explains why your coffee turned out sour or bitter, and [Extraction](https://ailabhub-directory.com/product.php?id=3275) builds the next recipe from your best attempts. [Coffee Notes](https://ailabhub-directory.com/product.php?id=3276) offers settings from people with the same grinder and machine, and [Beanconqueror](https://ailabhub-directory.com/product.php?id=3278) connects Bluetooth scales and other devices.

Equipment is getting smarter too. [Meticulous Espresso](https://ailabhub-directory.com/product.php?id=3302) is a robotic espresso machine with roaster profiles. [xBloom](https://ailabhub-directory.com/product.php?id=3304) automates pour-over from bean to cup, and [Decent Espresso](https://ailabhub-directory.com/product.php?id=3303) and the open-source [Gaggiuino](https://ailabhub-directory.com/product.php?id=3281) project offer detailed pressure profiling. More familiar options include [JURA](https://ailabhub-directory.com/product.php?id=3307), [Terra Kaffe](https://ailabhub-directory.com/product.php?id=3305), [La Marzocco](https://ailabhub-directory.com/product.php?id=3308), [Ratio Coffee](https://ailabhub-directory.com/product.php?id=3313) and [Acaia](https://ailabhub-directory.com/product.php?id=3279) scales.

For roasters there is [Cropster](https://ailabhub-directory.com/product.php?id=3282) with Roast AI, [Roast Master](https://ailabhub-directory.com/product.php?id=3287) with an AI coach, the open-source [Artisan](https://ailabhub-directory.com/product.php?id=3283) software and roasters from [Aillio](https://ailabhub-directory.com/product.php?id=3285), [IKAWA](https://ailabhub-directory.com/product.php?id=3284) and [Bellwether Coffee](https://ailabhub-directory.com/product.php?id=3286). [Trade Coffee](https://ailabhub-directory.com/product.php?id=3296) and [Bottomless](https://ailabhub-directory.com/product.php?id=3298) help you find coffee for your taste: the Bottomless scale orders a new bag by itself. You can learn the profession at [Barista Hustle](https://ailabhub-directory.com/product.php?id=3293) and the [Specialty Coffee Association](https://ailabhub-directory.com/product.php?id=3294).

## Wine: an AI sommelier in your pocket

Most often AI in wine helps with choosing. [Sommo](https://ailabhub-directory.com/product.php?id=3335) knows what is in your cellar and picks a bottle for dinner. [VinoVoss](https://ailabhub-directory.com/product.php?id=3336) finds wine from a plain-language description, [Hello Vino](https://ailabhub-directory.com/product.php?id=3343) suggests a bottle for a dish and an occasion, and [VinoLens](https://ailabhub-directory.com/product.php?id=3331) reads a photo of a wine list to help you choose in a restaurant. [Vivino](https://ailabhub-directory.com/product.php?id=3338), [Delectable](https://ailabhub-directory.com/product.php?id=3339), [CorkAI](https://ailabhub-directory.com/product.php?id=3333) and [WineDiscovery](https://ailabhub-directory.com/product.php?id=3334) scan labels. For collectors there is [CellarTracker](https://ailabhub-directory.com/product.php?id=3340) with AI recommendations and [InVintory](https://ailabhub-directory.com/product.php?id=3345) with 3D bottle finding.

For business AI works differently. [Sommelier.bot](https://ailabhub-directory.com/product.php?id=3337) adds an AI sommelier to a wine shop's website, [Preferabli](https://ailabhub-directory.com/product.php?id=3342) and [Tastry](https://ailabhub-directory.com/product.php?id=3347) model taste preferences, and [InnoVint](https://ailabhub-directory.com/product.php?id=3348), [vintrace](https://ailabhub-directory.com/product.php?id=3349) and [Commerce7](https://ailabhub-directory.com/product.php?id=3353) help wineries with production and sales. You can study with [Wine Folly](https://ailabhub-directory.com/product.php?id=3344), [WSET](https://ailabhub-directory.com/product.php?id=3365) and the [Court of Master Sommeliers Americas](https://ailabhub-directory.com/product.php?id=3366).

## Beer: from homebrewing to the brewery

For homebrewers AI helps most with the recipe and fermentation. [Brewfather](https://ailabhub-directory.com/product.php?id=3383) recalculates recipe stats in real time and turns the recipe into a checklist with timers, while [BeerSmith](https://ailabhub-directory.com/product.php?id=3384) and [Brewtarget](https://ailabhub-directory.com/product.php?id=3386) are classic recipe programs. [Tilt Hydrometer](https://ailabhub-directory.com/product.php?id=3388) and [iSpindel](https://ailabhub-directory.com/product.php?id=3389) sensors send gravity and temperature, and [Fermentrack](https://ailabhub-directory.com/product.php?id=3391) and [Brewblox](https://ailabhub-directory.com/product.php?id=3390) control fermentation.

If you do not want to get into the details, you can choose an all-in-one machine: [iGulu](https://ailabhub-directory.com/product.php?id=3393), [Brewart](https://ailabhub-directory.com/product.php?id=3394), [MiniBrew](https://ailabhub-directory.com/product.php?id=3395), [Pinter](https://ailabhub-directory.com/product.php?id=3396) or [Grainfather](https://ailabhub-directory.com/product.php?id=3392). Breweries will find [PLAATO](https://ailabhub-directory.com/product.php?id=3387) with AI production analytics, [Ekos](https://ailabhub-directory.com/product.php?id=3399), [Breww](https://ailabhub-directory.com/product.php?id=3401) and [Arryved](https://ailabhub-directory.com/product.php?id=3400) useful.

Beer lovers can use [Sipstr](https://ailabhub-directory.com/product.php?id=3380), which predicts whether you will like a new beer, [Brewzy](https://ailabhub-directory.com/product.php?id=3376) with AI recommendations, and the [Untappd](https://ailabhub-directory.com/product.php?id=3373) and [BeerAdvocate](https://ailabhub-directory.com/product.php?id=3374) communities.

## Whisky and other spirits

[MaltIQ](https://ailabhub-directory.com/product.php?id=3420) scans a shelf in a store or bar and suggests which bottle to try based on your taste. [Whiskey ID](https://ailabhub-directory.com/product.php?id=3412) recognizes whiskey, bourbon, gin, rum, brandy and vodka, and [Uncasked](https://ailabhub-directory.com/product.php?id=3414) estimates a bottle's market value. ## Running a bar: inventory, pours and forecasts

In a bar money is often lost unnoticed: an extra pour, inaccurate counts, overpaying a supplier. AI services help you see it. [WISK](https://ailabhub-directory.com/product.php?id=3426) combines inventory and purchasing and forecasts sales using weather, events and holidays. [Craftable](https://ailabhub-directory.com/product.php?id=3428) automatically matches invoices to orders, and [MarketMan](https://ailabhub-directory.com/product.php?id=3431) and [Apicbase](https://ailabhub-directory.com/product.php?id=3432) automate counting and costing.

For fast inventory there are [BinWise](https://ailabhub-directory.com/product.php?id=3421), [Backbar](https://ailabhub-directory.com/product.php?id=3427), [BevSpot](https://ailabhub-directory.com/product.php?id=3424), [Bar-i](https://ailabhub-directory.com/product.php?id=3425) and [Partender](https://ailabhub-directory.com/product.php?id=3422). [BarVision](https://ailabhub-directory.com/product.php?id=3272) and [Berg Liquor Controls](https://ailabhub-directory.com/product.php?id=3273) control pours, and [iPourIt](https://ailabhub-directory.com/product.php?id=3270) and [PourMyBeer](https://ailabhub-directory.com/product.php?id=3271) systems let guests pour for themselves. [SevenRooms](https://ailabhub-directory.com/product.php?id=3433) manages reservations and tables with AI, and [Provi](https://ailabhub-directory.com/product.php?id=3429) simplifies ordering from distributors. You can learn bartending at [BarSmarts](https://ailabhub-directory.com/product.php?id=3435) and the [European Bartender School](https://ailabhub-directory.com/product.php?id=3436).

## Robots behind the bar

Robot bartenders and robot baristas already work, though not everywhere. [Makr Shakr](https://ailabhub-directory.com/product.php?id=3261) robots mix cocktails, including on cruise ships. [Artly](https://ailabhub-directory.com/product.php?id=3265) and [Cafe X](https://ailabhub-directory.com/product.php?id=3269) run coffee shops where robots make the coffee, and [Richtech Robotics](https://ailabhub-directory.com/product.php?id=3263) built the ADAM robot barista. [Doosan Robotics](https://ailabhub-directory.com/product.php?id=3264) has shown a robot bartender that picks a cocktail based on a guest's facial expression. [Botrista](https://ailabhub-directory.com/product.php?id=3262) forecasts beverage demand and prepares drinks automatically, [Blendid](https://ailabhub-directory.com/product.php?id=3267) makes smoothies in an autonomous kiosk, and [RobotAnno](https://ailabhub-directory.com/product.php?id=3266) sells robotic kiosks for coffee, tea and cocktails.

## How to choose a tool

- **At home:** start with a free cocktail generator or espresso journal, and buy a gadget once you know what you really need.
- **For baristas:** a shot journal with AI tips saves beans, and a Bluetooth scale makes the data more accurate.
- **For bartenders:** inventory with POS integration pays off faster than a robot.
- **Check prices and subscriptions:** many apps are only partly free.
- **Check AI advice:** a recipe from a neural network can be unbalanced, so trust your own taste.

## Drink responsibly

AI will suggest a recipe, but it will not count how much you have already had. Remember that alcohol harms your health, and you must not drive after drinking. The non-alcoholic recipes in this article are just as tasty, so choose them when you need to stay sober.
