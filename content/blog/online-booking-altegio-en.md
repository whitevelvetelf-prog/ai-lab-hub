# Online Booking for Clients: Altegio and Other Services for Salons and Specialists

**In short:** online booking takes calls and messages off the specialist's or administrator's plate: clients pick the service, specialist and free slot themselves, and the system reminds them about the visit. [Altegio](product:Altegio) is a full system for businesses with several staff or branches: booking, CRM, finance, payroll and inventory in one place. If you work alone or run a small studio, simpler apps may suit you better; we compare them below.

> **Important.** We checked prices and features against official websites and app descriptions on October 30, 2026. Plans change, so check the current price on the service's website before paying.

## What online booking gives you

- **24/7 booking.** Clients book from your website, social media or a messenger, even while you are working or asleep.
- **Fewer no-shows.** Automatic reminders by SMS, messengers or push notifications.
- **A tidy schedule.** No two clients in the same slot and no bookings lost in notes.
- **Client history.** You see who had what done and when they last came, so it is easier to invite them back.

## Altegio: what it can do

[Altegio](product:Altegio) is a platform for beauty salons, clinics, fitness studios, schools, car services and other appointment-based businesses. ALTEGIO LTD describes itself as a European IT holding; it has a Ukrainian version of its website and apps, and according to the website, data is stored in the EU.

- **Online booking** from a website, app, messengers and social media.
- **Reminders** via WhatsApp, SMS, email and push.
- **CRM:** visit history, no-shows, memberships, a loyalty programme.
- **Finance and accounting:** cash flow, payroll, inventory of consumables, statistics by branch.
- **Altegio.me client app:** clients see all their bookings and can reschedule, buy a membership or a gift certificate. Altegio offers it instead of a separate branded app.
- **Connecting an AI assistant.** Through Altegio's MCP server you can connect Claude, ChatGPT or Cursor and, in a chat, find clients, reschedule bookings and look at your numbers, within your account's permissions.

## Other online booking services

**[Bookon CRM](product:Bookon CRM)** is a system from the Ukrainian company Binotel for beauty salons, barbershops and other service businesses. 24/7 booking from your website and social media, an appointment book with access for each specialist, a client profile with visit history, calls and SMS straight from the profile, and analytics by specialist. You can manage bookings from the Bookon Biz mobile app. According to the Binotel website, the Bookon licence costs UAH 750/month for a salon with 5 specialists, and Bookon One costs UAH 375/month for up to 2.

**[Sprava](product:Sprava)** is a Ukrainian app for specialists and small salons. You add a booking link to Instagram, and clients choose the service, specialist and time themselves. It has SMS reminders, client history and notes, team schedules and earnings tracking.

**[bety](product:bety)** is a Kyiv-made app for beauty professionals. Each specialist gets a mini-site with 24/7 booking; clients can attach a reference photo, reschedule or cancel. There is prepayment, which cuts last-minute cancellations, reminders via a Telegram bot and SMS, feedback collection and rebooking invitations.

**[PYFO](product:PYFO)** is an app and CRM for beauty professionals with a personal booking page. Besides the calendar, it has a directory of specialists: clients search by rating, location and work, and specialists can post a portfolio and collect reviews.

**[ShineBooking](product:ShineBooking)** serves both sides: clients find specialists nearby on a map, watch video profiles and reviews and book in two taps, while specialists get a personal site, a calendar without double bookings, a client base, income analytics and sync with Google and Apple Calendar.

## Booking a doctor
