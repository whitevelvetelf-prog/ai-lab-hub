# Game Recording Software: Insights Capture and Alternatives

**In short:** [Insights Capture](product:Insights Capture) automatically records matches in 10,000+ games and lets you review the recordings with your team or coach. It is the best choice if you want not just to save moments but to analyze your play. If your main goal is to share highlight clips quickly or to stream, there are handier options, covered below.

## What Insights Capture does

It is a Windows app. It detects the game and starts recording on its own, with no scenes or codecs to set up. Clips are saved in two clicks with no frame loss. For coaches and teams there is recording review (VOD): you can watch matches back, comment on them and analyze them in Valorant, League of Legends, Counter-Strike 2, Apex Legends, Rainbow 6 Siege, Fortnite and other games.

**Pricing:** the basic version is free, with 5 GB of storage. The Silver plan at $39 a month gives 5 seats for Elite users and 200 GB. Paid plans also add ad-free use, more storage and advanced settings, and team bundles cost less. See current prices on [Insights Capture](product:Insights Capture).

**Who it is for:** gamers, streamers, esports coaches and teams.

## Comparison with alternatives

**[Medal](product:Medal)** automatically detects kills, wins and other highlights and saves them as clips. Insights Capture instead relies on recording the whole session and manual bookmarks for review. Medal also has the largest public clip community. Choose Medal if you want to share moments quickly, and Insights Capture if you need in-depth analysis.

**[Outplayed](product:Outplayed)** also creates clips automatically, but it runs on the [Overwolf](product:Overwolf) platform and will not start without it, while Insights Capture is fully standalone. It suits people who already use the Overwolf app suite.

**[OBS Studio](product:OBS Studio)** is a free open-source program with full manual control, and it works for streaming too. The downside: first you need scenes, capture sources, a codec choice and test recordings. For serious broadcasts it is the best choice; for quick game recording it is too complicated.

**[NVIDIA ShadowPlay](product:NVIDIA App)** is a lightweight replay buffer for GeForce graphics card owners. Handy if you have an NVIDIA card, but it offers no tools for match review.

**[Xbox Game Bar](product:Xbox Game Bar)** is already built into Windows and needs no installation. It is fine for occasional clips, but it is sometimes unstable: widgets do not open, and hotkeys can disappear after updates.
