## 1. Generating video from text or an image

A generator takes a scene description (prompt) or an image and returns a clip a few seconds long. You won’t get a long film from one request: videos are built from clips and then edited together.

**What to try first:**

- [Runway](product:Runway) — one of the AI video pioneers: text and image to video, motion transfer, editing existing footage and a set of editing tools in the browser.
- [Kling AI](product:Kling AI) — strong at realistic human and animal motion, with image-to-video modes and lip sync.
- [Google Veo](product:Google Veo) — Google’s model that generates video together with sound. Available in Google AI plans; to assemble scenes into a film there is [Google Flow](product:Google Flow).
- [Hailuo AI](product:Hailuo AI), [Pika](product:Pika), [PixVerse](product:PixVerse), [Vidu](product:Vidu) and [Luma Dream Machine](product:Luma Dream Machine) — handy for trying for free and making short social clips.
- [Higgsfield](product:Higgsfield) — music-video-style effects and camera moves in a few clicks.
- [Seedance](product:Seedance) — ByteDance’s video model, good at keeping one character consistent across several shots.

**What about Sora?** OpenAI shut down the Sora app and website on 26 April 2026 and the Sora API on 24 September 2026. If you see “free Sora” or “Sora with no waitlist”, that is a third-party site, not OpenAI. Be careful with payments and personal data.

**How to write a video prompt:**

1. **Who does what:** “a ginger cat walks slowly along a windowsill”.
2. **Where and when:** “an old Kyiv apartment, morning, soft window light”.
3. **Camera:** “slow push-in”, “eye-level camera”, “drone shot”.
4. **Style:** “cinematic, 35 mm”, “anime”, “clay stop-motion”.
5. **One action per clip.** The more happens in a prompt, the more often the model gets confused.

The most reliable route is **image first, then video**: generate a frame in [Midjourney](product:Midjourney) or [Adobe Firefly](product:Adobe Firefly) (or use your own photo) and animate it in a generator. That keeps the character and style from drifting between clips. More on image generation in [“Midjourney alternatives”](blog-post.php?slug=midjourney-alternatives).

## 2. AI editing: pauses, captions, short clips

This is where AI saves the most time, even if you generate nothing and just film yourself on a phone.

- **Edit by text.** [Descript](product:Descript) transcribes your video and you edit it like a document: delete a sentence in the text and it disappears from the video. It removes filler words and pauses and evens out the sound.
- **Rough cut for YouTubers.** [Gling](product:Gling) cuts bad takes, silences and “um”s by itself, adds captions and exports the edit to Premiere, Final Cut or DaVinci Resolve. Free gives 1 hour a month with a watermark; paid plans start at $10 a month billed annually.
- **Short clips from long videos.** [OpusClip](product:OpusClip), [Vizard](product:Vizard) and [Submagic](product:Submagic) find the best moments in a long video, reframe them vertically and add dynamic captions.
- **Captions and translation in the browser.** [VEED](product:VEED) and [Kapwing](product:Kapwing) do captions, translation and quick edits with nothing to install.
- **On your phone.** [CapCut](product:CapCut) is the most popular mobile editor with auto captions, templates and AI effects. Simpler options are [InShot](product:InShot) and [VN Video Editor](product:VN Video Editor).
- **Podcasts and interviews.** [Riverside.fm](product:Riverside.fm) records each participant on a separate high-quality track and then cuts clips for you.
- **Full editing software.** [DaVinci Resolve](product:DaVinci Resolve) is free and professional, with AI features for audio, colour and masks. [Kdenlive](product:Kdenlive) and [Shotcut](product:Shotcut) are open source and free. Paid industry standards are [Adobe Premiere Pro](product:Adobe Premiere Pro), and [Filmora](product:Filmora) for simpler work.

> **Save your toolkit.** Most creators combine 2–3 tools: a generator, an editor and a clipper. [Create an account](register.php?ref=blog-ai-video) and keep them in your saved list to compare plans and come back when a free limit runs out.

## 3. Video without a camera: avatars and voiceover

When you don’t want to be on camera or need a training video in several languages:

- **Avatar presenters.** [HeyGen](product:HeyGen), [Synthesia](product:Synthesia) and [Colossyan](product:Colossyan) turn text into video with a digital presenter. You can also create your own avatar from a recording of yourself. For a talking photo, try [D-ID](product:D-ID) and [Hedra](product:Hedra).
- **Video from text or an article.** [Fliki](product:Fliki), [InVideo](product:InVideo) and [Pictory](product:Pictory) split text into scenes, pick stock footage and add a voiceover. Fliki has a free plan: 3 credits a month, 720p and a watermark.
- **Voiceover and dubbing.** [ElevenLabs](product:ElevenLabs) offers realistic voices in many languages, cloning your own voice and dubbing videos into other languages. More tools in [Voiceover](subcategory:voiceover).

## 4. Open models: generate on your own computer

With a powerful NVIDIA GPU you can generate video locally: no subscription, limits or queues, and your files stay on your machine.

- [Wan](product:Wan) from Alibaba: versions 2.1 and 2.2 were released with open weights under Apache 2.0. Newer versions run on the wan.video platform and via API.
- [HunyuanVideo](product:HunyuanVideo) from Tencent is an open model with 13+ billion parameters, with image-to-video, avatar and lighter 1.5 versions. The original version needs 45–60 GB of GPU memory.
- [LTX-2](product:LTX-2) from Lightricks generates video with sound in one pass, can regenerate a single segment and dub speech while keeping lip movement. The weights use their own community licence.
- [ComfyUI](product:ComfyUI) is a visual interface for wiring these models into pipelines without code.

An honest note on hardware: this won’t run on a regular laptop. To start you need a GPU with 12–24 GB of memory and lighter model versions. Without one, cloud services work out cheaper.

## What it costs

- **Free:** almost every generator gives a few tries a day or month, usually with a watermark and lower quality. [DaVinci Resolve](product:DaVinci Resolve), [Kdenlive](product:Kdenlive), [Shotcut](product:Shotcut) and open models are free without limits.
- **$10–30 a month** is a typical entry plan for a generator or AI editor: more credits, 1080p, no watermark, commercial use.
- **Credits expire.** Unused credits usually don’t roll over. Don’t buy an annual plan before testing the free tier.
- **One second is not one try.** A good 5-second clip often takes 3–10 generations. Budget for that.

## Rules, copyright and honesty

- **Label AI video.** YouTube requires disclosure of realistic altered or synthetic content; TikTok and Meta ask for an AI label. Unlabelled videos can be restricted or removed.
- **Don’t generate real people without consent.** A video of a celebrity or someone you know saying what they never said is a deepfake and can get you banned or sued. How to spot other people’s deepfakes and protect yourself: [“How to protect yourself from scams and deepfakes”](blog-post.php?slug=ai-fraud-deepfakes-protection).
- **Commercial use only under your plan’s terms.** Many free plans forbid commercial use. For client ads choose a paid plan or models trained on licensed data, such as [Moonvalley Marey](product:Moonvalley Marey) or [Adobe Firefly](product:Adobe Firefly).
- **Music and voices.** Don’t clone someone else’s voice or use unlicensed music: that breaks copyright even if the video is generated.

## FAQ

**What is the best free AI video generator?**
There is no single one: free limits change often. Start with [Hailuo AI](product:Hailuo AI), [Pika](product:Pika) or [PixVerse](product:PixVerse), and with a powerful GPU try the open [Wan](product:Wan) or [LTX-2](product:LTX-2).

**Can I make a long video from one prompt?**
No. Generators make clips of 5–10 seconds (some longer). Long videos are assembled from clips in an editor.

**Do these tools work in languages other than English?**
Interfaces are mostly in English, and prompts work most reliably in English. Voiceover and captions in many languages are supported by [ElevenLabs](product:ElevenLabs), [CapCut](product:CapCut), [VEED](product:VEED) and others; check the quality on the free plan.
