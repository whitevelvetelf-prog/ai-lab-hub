# Dog training apps: lessons, clicker and puppy routines

Bringing home a puppy or an adult rescue dog is a joy and a lot of questions: how to potty train, teach loose-leash walking and help the dog stay calm at home alone. Training apps won't replace a real trainer, but they help you practice regularly, in small steps and with a plan. Here is what is out there.

All the tools in this article are collected in the "Dog training" subcategory of the "My Home" section of our directory.

> **Important.** Training methods vary. Choose methods that avoid pain and fear. Serious behavior problems (aggression, severe anxiety) are best discussed with a trainer or a veterinarian.

## In short: what these apps do

- **Daily programs** — short exercises for every day.
- **Video courses** — step-by-step lessons for commands and tricks.
- **Puppy plans** — training by age and breed.
- **Routine logs** — potty, feeding, walks and medication.
- **Clicker and whistle** — tools right on your phone.

## Daily programs and video lessons

Most apps are based on positive reinforcement: the dog gets a reward for the right behavior. [Dogo](https://ailabhub-directory.com/product.php?id=822) offers daily training tasks, video instructions and a built-in clicker, and according to its descriptions you can send a training video and get feedback from a trainer. [Puppr](https://ailabhub-directory.com/product.php?id=2743) has more than a hundred video lessons from trainer Sara Carson, with a strong focus on tricks.

[Pupford](https://ailabhub-directory.com/product.php?id=2742) is a large library of video courses; according to reviews, the basic 30-day course is free and deeper material is paid. [Hundeo](https://ailabhub-directory.com/product.php?id=3558) builds a personal plan and shows one exercise a day, while [PawChamp](https://ailabhub-directory.com/product.php?id=3556) combines training, behavior and care in one plan with expert chat. [Woofz](https://ailabhub-directory.com/product.php?id=2748) brings together lessons and a daily care log.

## For puppies

The first months matter most for socialization and habits. [Zigzag](https://ailabhub-directory.com/product.php?id=3555) is entirely about puppies: short lessons by breed and age and, according to the developers, a 12-week puppy development program. Routine logs keep you on track: [Pup to Date](https://ailabhub-directory.com/product.php?id=3559) shows when the puppy last went out, ate or had medication and has an Apple Watch app, and [Doggy Time](https://ailabhub-directory.com/product.php?id=3557) records potty, walks, feeding and training and lets you share records with family.

## Clicker and whistle on your phone

Clicker training works like this: the click marks the exact moment of the right behavior, then the dog gets a reward. [iTrainer Dog Whistle & Clicker](https://ailabhub-directory.com/product.php?id=3560) is a free clicker and whistle for iPhone with adjustable frequency and Apple Watch support. [Dogo](https://ailabhub-directory.com/product.php?id=822) has a built-in clicker too.

## How to choose

1. **Define the goal.** A puppy and basic manners, tricks or a specific problem — each needs a different app.
2. **Look at the methods.** Choose programs based on positive reinforcement, without pain, fear or punishment.
3. **Consistency beats duration.** A few minutes every day achieve more than an hour once a week.
4. **Know the limits.** If your dog shows aggression, strong fear or a sudden change in behavior, see a trainer or a veterinarian: sometimes the cause is health-related.
